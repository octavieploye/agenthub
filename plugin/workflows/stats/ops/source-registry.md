# SOURCE REGISTRY — Verified Statistical Sources by Domain

This registry lists the primary verified sources the stats team draws from.
Sources are organized by domain. Trust tier (T0–T5) is pre-assigned.
Always verify the specific publication year when citing — this registry lists the institution, not a specific edition.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS PRIMARY SOURCES (T2)

Key works with confirmed replication status:
  Kahneman & Tversky (1979) — Prospect Theory: Very High replication
  Thaler & Benartzi (2004) — Save More Tomorrow: High replication
  Madrian & Shea (2001) — 401k Default Effect: High replication
  Iyengar & Lepper (2000) — Choice Overload: Moderate (replication mixed)
  Ariely (2008) — Predictably Irrational: Moderate (some effects not fully replicated)
  Barber & Odean (2001) — Trading and Overconfidence: High replication

Always check the Open Science Foundation replication database for replication status of specific studies.
URL: osf.io — search by paper title or author.
Replication status dramatically affects CS assignment.

## SOURCES TO USE WITH CAUTION

Not prohibited, but require explicit flagging and reduced CS:
  - Press releases from companies about market data (conflict of interest: -15 CS)
  - Vendor-sponsored research reports (conflict of interest: -15 CS)
  - Pre-print papers (SSRN, arXiv) not yet peer-reviewed (T5 treatment until published)
  - Social media aggregated statistics (treat as T5 maximum)
  - AI-generated statistics without original source citation (PROHIBITED — never cite)
