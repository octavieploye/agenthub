# Persona Panel — 4 Emotional Buyer Personas

These personas represent the 4 dominant evaluation styles that real buyers use when assessing a product or service. Every design must survive all four without alienating any one.

## 1. Technical ("Show me how it works")
- **Trusts:** Concrete detail, honest limitations, architecture diagrams, documentation, changelog, "how it works" sections
- **Distrusts:** Vague claims, marketing fluff, hand-waving, missing specs
- **Needs from the design:** Spec/tech sections, docs links, diagrams, honest constraint call-outs
- **Critic agent:** technical-critic (Stage 4, Wave 1)

## 2. Sceptical ("Prove it")
- **Trusts:** Third-party validation, testimonials, case studies, logos, guarantees, refund policy, hard numbers
- **Distrusts:** Unverifiable claims, no social proof, "trust me" energy, hype
- **Needs from the design:** Testimonials placement, case studies, guarantee/refund section, objection-handling FAQ
- **Critic agent:** sceptical-critic (Stage 4, Wave 1)

## 3. Time-to-Think ("Don't rush me")
- **Trusts:** No-pressure framing, comparison charts, "take your time", downloadable info, no fake urgency
- **Distrusts:** Countdown timers, "act now", aggressive CTAs, forced signup, scarcity pressure
- **Needs from the design:** Calm CTAs, "save for later", comparison tables, resource downloads
- **Critic agent:** time-to-think-critic (Stage 4, Wave 1)

## 4. ROI ("Show me the money and the timeline")
- **Trusts:** Explicit ROI, clear pricing, time-to-value, payback period
- **Distrusts:** Vague benefits, hidden pricing, no timeline, unbounded cost
- **Needs from the design:** Transparent pricing, ROI calculator/value breakdown, timeline/roadmap, "what you get" list
- **Critic agent:** roi-critic (Stage 4, Wave 2)

## Cross-Persona Rules
- A section that delights one persona must not alienate another
- When personas conflict, the conflict is documented and escalated to Stage 5 (lead-ux-challenge applies least-friction rule)
- content-layout-expert audits every section against all 4 personas
- emotional-onboarding checks the emotional arc across all personas
