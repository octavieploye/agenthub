# Market Modeling — Workflow Manifest
*Five knowledge traditions. Four phases. No point predictions. No corporate ground truth.*

---

## The Team

Seven cognitive roles across four phases:

| Role | Cognitive stance | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Harvester A | Academic & quantitative — NBER, arXiv, JSTOR, SSRN, JASSS | 1 |
| Harvester B | Official & institutional — IMF, OECD, BIS, ECB, World Bank, Eurostat | 1 |
| Harvester C | Heterodox & complexity — INET Oxford, Santa Fe, PKSE, Levy, Stockholm | 1 |
| Analyst | Five-lens analysis across all traditions | 2 |
| Shadow | Adversarial review — seven questions, no softening | 3 |
| Synthesiser | Convergence mapping — what holds, what diverges, what was not asked | 4 |
| Scribe | Standard output — no verdicts, no confidence percentages | 4 |

Full role definitions: `team.md`

---

## The Sequence

```
Phase 1 — Parallel Harvest (Harvesters A, B, C simultaneously)
         ↓ Gate 1: ≥5 non-corporate sources, all three traditions represented
Phase 2 — Five-Lens Analysis (Analyst)
         ↓ Gate 2: all five lenses applied, cross-lens observations complete
Phase 3 — Shadow Adversarial Review (Shadow)
         ↓ Gate 3: all seven questions answered with specificity
Phase 4 — Synthesis + Standard Output (Synthesiser → Scribe)
         ↓ Gate 4: shadow integrated, convergence mapped, output complete
              Human receives the intelligence
```

A phase does not advance until its gate passes. Gate checklists are in `handoffs.md`.

---

## The Five Lenses

| Lens | What it sees | What it structurally cannot see |
|---|---|---|
| Equilibrium (PE/CGE) | Supply-demand structure, elasticities, welfare | Endogenous crises, heterogeneous behavior |
| Dynamic (DSGE/VAR) | Shock transmission, impulse responses, forecasts | Path dependence, distribution, fragility |
| Agent-Based / Complexity | Emergent phenomena, lock-in, boom-bust, herding | Structural prediction at turning points |
| Post-Keynesian / Minsky | Financial fragility, monopoly pricing, sectoral balances | Efficient market allocation |
| Ecological / True Cost | Externalized costs, throughput, planetary limits | Conventional optimality |

---

## The Convergence Rule

A finding is **load-bearing** when confirmed by sources from at least 3 of the 5 traditions. State this explicitly: `[CONVERGENT — N traditions agree]`. If fewer than 5 non-corporate sources returned, flag: `[BELOW CONVERGENCE THRESHOLD — N sources]`.

---

## What This Workflow Does Not Do

- Does not recommend investment decisions
- Does not produce marketing copy or product positioning
- Does not replace Demiurge thinking — it feeds it
- Does not declare convergence before earning it
- Does not skip the Shadow step because the analysis looks clean
- Does not produce output formatted as a corporate research report
