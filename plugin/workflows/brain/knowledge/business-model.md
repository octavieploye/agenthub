## PHASE 1 — STANDALONE PRODUCTS (current)

Three independent products, each monetizable on its own before the ecosystem converges.

### Product A: agenthub (Hephaestus)
What:     Desktop app for orchestrating multiple AI coding agents simultaneously.
          The builder's control room. Multiple Claude CLI sessions, kanban, TTS, Business Mode.
Audience: Developers and small teams who use Claude CLI for coding.
Model:    Currently internal / portfolio. Potential: commercial license or open-core.
Distribution: Direct download / GitHub releases.

### Product B: llm-workflows-pckg (@optimaeus/agent-workflows)
What:     Installable TypeScript workflow guardrails library. Schemas, runtime engine,
          CLI for enforcing structured AI workflows.
Audience: Developers who want structured, auditable AI agent workflows in their projects.
Distribution (3 channels):
  1. npx install / npm package — developer-direct
  2. Installer (desktop companion for non-coders)
  3. Opeidos marketplace — curated workflow packages
Model:    Free core library (open source or source-available). Premium workflows via Opeidos.

### Product C: optimaeus HEAD (personal assistant / second brain)
What:     Electron app. Constellation view of all your projects. Philosophical reckoning.
          Personal AI assistant that knows your full context.
Audience: Founders, builders, strategists who need a sovereign personal assistant.
Distribution: Desktop installer.
Model:    TBD — freemium (basic constellation + reckoning free, Anamnesis memory + team
          features subscription) OR one-time license.

## THE OPEIDOS MARKETPLACE

Brand:    Opeidos
Role:     Marketplace for installable AI workflow packages.
          Developers and teams publish, discover, and install structured workflows.
Access points:
  - npx @opeidos/[workflow-name] — single command install
  - Chrome/browser extension — discover workflows from any webpage or IDE
  - Desktop installer — offline-capable workflow manager
Monetization:
  FREE tier:   Business search workflow + Marketing strategy workflow
               (demonstrates the value of structured AI workflows vs. raw prompting)
  PAID tier:   Advanced workflows via subscription
               (deeper research layers, financial modeling, legal intelligence, etc.)
  CREATOR tier: Workflow authors publish and earn per install/subscription share

Sovereignty note: Opeidos is a marketplace for open, portable workflows.
It does not create lock-in. Any workflow installed via Opeidos runs locally.

## COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

What we are NOT:
  Not a ChatGPT wrapper. Not another prompt library. Not a no-code AI platform.
  Not dependent on any single LLM provider. Not locked to any cloud.

What we ARE:
  Structured AI workflows with audit trails and non-assumption rules.
  Sovereign infrastructure — your tools, your data, your brain.
  A system that gets smarter over time through Anamnesis — not through surveillance.

Target buyer:
  Phase 1: Individual founders and developers who waste hours in unstructured AI sessions.
  Phase 2: Small teams (3-20 people) who need shared AI context without giving up sovereignty.
  Phase 3: Sovereign-minded enterprises and governments (EU market first).
