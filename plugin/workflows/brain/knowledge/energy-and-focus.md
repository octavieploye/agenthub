## PURPOSE

Rules for managing energy, not just time. Time is fixed at 14-15h/day.
Energy is renewable — but only if managed. These rules determine WHEN
to place WHICH type of work.

---

## RULE 1 — ENERGY IS THE CURRENCY, NOT TIME

Time is fixed. Energy is renewable and manageable.
Managing time without managing energy is a losing strategy.

Four energy dimensions — all must be managed:

| Dimension | What it is | What depletes it | What renews it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physical | Sleep, nutrition, movement, hydration | Sleep debt, sitting all day, poor food | Exercise, sleep, meals, water |
| Emotional | Positive emotions, resilience, self-regulation | Conflict, isolation, anxiety | Connection, gratitude, laughter |
| Mental | Focus, creativity, concentration | Multitasking, context switching, decision overload | Single-tasking, breaks, flow state |
| Spiritual | Purpose, values alignment, motivation | Meaningless work, misalignment with values | Work that matters, clear purpose |

Physical energy is the foundation. If it breaks, all others collapse.

---

## RULE 2 — THE 90-MINUTE CYCLE (Ultradian Rhythm)

The human body cycles between high performance and renewal every 90-120 minutes.

```
[  90 min HIGH PERFORMANCE  ] → [ 20 min RENEWAL ] → [ 90 min HIGH PERFORMANCE ] → ...
```

| Phase | Duration | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| High performance | 90 min | Alertness, concentration, creativity peak | Deep work, maker tasks, hard decisions |
| Renewal | 20 min | Body needs recovery — energy dips | Walk, stretch, eat, look away from screen |

### Rules:
1. Work in 90-minute blocks maximum, then take a real break
2. Do NOT push through the renewal phase — it degrades the next cycle
3. A "break" is NOT checking email — it is physical movement or rest
4. Schedule 2-3 high-performance blocks per day = 3-4.5 hours of deep work
5. This is realistic maximum deep work — do not expect 8 hours of it

### Why this matters:
- Elite athletes spend 5x more time recovering than average athletes
- Same pattern holds in knowledge work
- Stress is not the enemy — insufficient recovery is the enemy
- Rituals (not willpower) sustain high performance

## RULE 3 — CONTEXT SWITCHING DESTROYS OUTPUT

Research data (HBR 2022):
- Knowledge workers toggle between apps/websites 1,200 times per day
- ~4 hours per week spent merely reorienting after switches
- Over a year: ~5 working weeks (~9% of annual work time) lost

### Rules:
1. Batch similar tasks together — do all writing in one block, all meetings in another
2. Do NOT alternate between deep work and email/chat
3. Close all communication tools during maker blocks
4. Each context switch costs 15-25 minutes to regain full focus
5. Three switches in a morning = the morning is gone

### Cost table:

| Switches per morning | Effective deep work hours (of 4h block) |
|---|---|
| 0 | 4.0h |
| 1 | 2.5-3.0h |
| 2 | 1.5-2.0h |
| 3+ | <1.0h — morning is effectively lost |

## RULE 4 — DECISION FATIGUE IS REAL

Humans make ~30,000 decisions per day. Each one depletes cognitive reserves.

Research: Israeli parole board study — approval rates drop from ~65% at
session start to near 0% before breaks, then reset after food/rest.

### Decision quality across the day:

| Time of day | Decision quality | What to schedule |
|---|---|---|
| First 2-3 hours | Highest | Hard decisions, strategy, IPAs |
| Mid-day | Declining | Routine decisions, structured work |
| Late afternoon | Lowest | Admin, pre-decided tasks, email |

### Countermeasures:

| Strategy | How it works | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Standard routines | Eliminate trivial decisions | Same wake time, go-to breakfast, standard outfits |
| Front-load hard decisions | Use peak hours for what matters most | Strategy calls at 9am, not 4pm |
| Decision batching | Group similar decisions into one session | All hiring decisions on Tuesday morning |
| Default rules | Pre-decide entire categories | "I always decline dinner meetings", "No calls before 9am" |
| Reduce options | Fewer choices = less fatigue | 3 options max for any decision |

## RULE 5 — MATCH TASK TYPE TO ENERGY LEVEL

| Energy level | Task type | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Peak (first 2-3h) | Maker work, IPAs, hard decisions | Writing, coding, strategy, product design |
| High (next 2-3h) | Structured creative work | Content creation, planning, analysis |
| Medium (post-lunch) | Manager work, collaboration | Meetings, reviews, team coordination |
| Declining (late afternoon) | Routine, pre-decided tasks | Email, admin, data entry, scheduling |
| Low (end of day) | Planning and rituals | Shutdown ritual, plan next day, weekly review |

### Rules:
1. NEVER put maker work in declining energy slots
2. NEVER put email/admin in peak energy slots
3. If you must take a meeting during peak energy, switch entire block to manager mode
4. Use the 90-minute cycle within each energy level

---

## RULE 6 — RECOVERY IS PRODUCTIVE

Recovery is not wasted time. It is what makes the next performance cycle possible.

| Recovery type | When | Duration | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro-recovery | Between 90-min cycles | 20 min | Walk, stretch, water, look away from screen |
| Meal break | Midday | 30-45 min | Eat away from desk, no screens |
| Transition buffer | Between task types | 10-15 min | Clear desk, close apps, set intention for next block |
| End-of-day shutdown | End of work | 15-20 min | Capture open loops, plan tomorrow, close work mentally |
| Weekly renewal | Weekend | ~4h minimum | Non-work activities, social, nature, hobbies |

### Rules:
1. Build recovery into the calendar as HARD blocks — not optional fillers
2. A blocked recovery slot is as important as a client meeting
3. Skipping recovery for one day is manageable — skipping for a week degrades everything
4. Physical recovery (sleep, movement) is a PREREQUISITE to cognitive performance, not a reward

## QUICK REFERENCE — ENERGY-OPTIMIZED DAY

```
[Peak]     06:00-09:00  →  Deep work block 1 (90 min + 20 min break + 90 min)
[Break]    09:00-09:30  →  Micro-recovery
[High]     09:30-12:00  →  Deep work block 2 or structured creative work
[Meal]     12:00-12:45  →  Lunch — away from desk
[Medium]   12:45-15:00  →  Manager block (meetings, calls, reviews)
[Break]    15:00-15:15  →  Transition buffer
[Declining]15:15-17:00  →  Admin, email, routine tasks
[Low]      17:00-17:20  →  Shutdown ritual — capture open loops, plan tomorrow
```

This is a template for a standard chronotype. Shift all blocks based
on the user's actual chronotype (see human-constraints.md).

## ANTI-PATTERNS

1. Expecting 8 hours of deep work per day — realistic maximum is 3-4.5 hours
2. Checking email during maker blocks "just for a second" — costs 15-25 min to refocus
3. Pushing through the 90-min renewal phase — degrades the next cycle
4. Scheduling hard decisions at 4pm — decision quality is at its lowest
5. Treating breaks as laziness — recovery enables the next performance cycle
6. Managing time but not energy — perfectly scheduled day with no fuel to execute
7. No transition buffer between task types — cognitive residue carries over
