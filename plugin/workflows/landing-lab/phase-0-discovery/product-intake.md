# Phase 0: Product Intake & Discovery
# Agent: product-researcher | Output: Product Discovery Brief

## Purpose

Map everything known about the product before any messaging, positioning, or copy work begins. Nothing is assumed. Everything is sourced from user-provided materials or searchable public presence.

## Intake Questions (lead-landing-lab asks user at session start)

Before dispatching product-researcher, lead collects:

1. What is the product / service / website? (name + one-line description)
2. What materials can you share? (URL, docs, README, About page, existing copy, research data)
3. Who do you believe your primary audience is? (note: this is a hypothesis — pain-mapper will validate)
4. What is the primary goal of this session? (new landing page / article set / onboarding copy / audit existing copy)
5. Do you have existing customer research, reviews, or feedback to share?

If materials are insufficient to complete all 6 sections of the Product Discovery Brief: create a Data Request List and pause until user provides what is needed.

---

## Research Protocol

Sources to check (in order of trust):
1. User-provided materials (highest trust — direct from source)
2. Official product website / landing page / About page
3. App Store / marketplace listings
4. Public documentation, README, or changelog
5. Press coverage from named publications (flag as `[EXTERNAL - not controlled by product]`)

Do NOT use: reviews aggregators, Reddit comments, social media as sources for product facts. Those are buyer signals for pain-mapper, not product facts.

---

## Product Discovery Brief Structure

### 1. Product / Service Core
- What it does (one sentence — plain language, not marketing)
- Category
- Problem it claims to solve
- Business model

### 2. Philosophy & Positioning
- Mission or founding story (with source)
- Core values or principles stated in materials
- Differentiation claims stated in materials
- Any sovereignty / privacy / ethical claims

### 3. Features & Functionalities
- Complete feature list from docs / product page
- Each feature: core / secondary / roadmap [UNCONFIRMED]
- Integrations, APIs, ecosystem dependencies

### 4. Documented Target Market
- Who the product explicitly targets per its own materials
- Any stated personas or ICP language
- Any "who this is NOT for" language

### 5. Marketplace & Distribution
- Where product is sold / found
- Any marketplace listings or category descriptions
- Nearest 3 competitor products (name + one-line positioning)

### 6. Credibility & Social Proof
- Founding team credentials mentioned in materials
- Customer quotes, case studies, or testimonials in materials
- Press, awards, or external validation documented

---

## Flagging Rules

- Every stated fact must have a source: `(source: product website)` / `(source: user-provided doc)`
- Every inference or assumption: `[UNVERIFIED — confirm with user]`
- Every competitor claim not from product materials: `[EXTERNAL CLAIM]`

---

## Confirmation Gate

After completing the Product Discovery Brief:
1. Lead presents the brief to the user
2. Lead asks: "Here is what we understand about your product. Is this accurate and complete? Please correct anything before we continue."
3. User must confirm or correct before Phase 1 begins
4. Any corrections update the brief before it is locked as the Phase 0 output
