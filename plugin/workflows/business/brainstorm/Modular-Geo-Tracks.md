  The Architecture Change: Modular Geo-Tracks
                                             
  The current FR / EU / US trio is hardcoded. The new system is modular — the user activates the geo-tracks relevant to each project at task creation. No
  project is forced to run all tracks. No track is silently excluded by default.                                                                          
   
  At task creation, user selects active geo-tracks:                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                        
  [ ] Global (always on — cannot be deactivated)                                                                                                          
  [ ] France (FR)
  [ ] European Union (EU)                                                                                                                                 
  [ ] United States (US)                                                                                                                                
  [ ] China (CN)                                                                                                                                          
  [ ] Asia — Broader (JP, KR, IN, SG, SE Asia)                                                                                                          
  [ ] Africa (choose: Pan-African / specific countries)                                                                                                   
  [ ] Oceania (AU / NZ / Pacific)
                                                                                                                                                          
  Minimum selection: Global + 1 region                                                                                                                    
  Maximum: all tracks active simultaneously                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                          
  Each activated geo-track runs as a parallel column in every layer. Deactivated tracks are not analyzed — but they are flagged if a global signal        
  originates from a deactivated region so the user knows they may be missing context.

  - Significant (changes the strategic conclusion per region)                                                                                             
  - A data error (one track has wrong information)

  China / Asia — Signal Sources and Intelligence Profile
                                                                                                                                                          
  China (CN)                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                          
  Regulatory / Policy:                                                                                                                                    
  - CSRC (China Securities Regulatory Commission) — securities, M&A, IPO approvals
  - SAMR (State Administration for Market Regulation) — antitrust, merger control, consumer protection                                                    
  - MIIT (Ministry of Industry and Information Technology) — tech regulation, data governance, telecom                                                  
  - NDRC (National Development and Reform Commission) — investment policy, strategic industry direction                                                   
  - CAC (Cyberspace Administration of China) — internet regulation, algorithm rules, data localization                                                    
  - People's Daily / Xinhua — official policy direction signals (what the state is endorsing or discouraging)                                             
  - PBOC (People's Bank of China) — fintech regulation, digital yuan, capital controls                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                          
  Market / Corporate:                                                                                                                                     
  - HKEX (Hong Kong Stock Exchange) — listing filings, cross-border M&A                                                                                   
  - CNIPA — China patent filings (extremely high volume, very directional for tech)                                                                       
  - Caixin / South China Morning Post — business intelligence, less filtered than state media                                                           
  - 36Kr / Leiphone — Chinese startup ecosystem tracking                                                                                                  
  - Crunchbase CN — funding rounds, acquisitions                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                          
  Key signal types unique to China:                                                                                                                       
  - Regulatory crackdown signals — sudden enforcement actions that reshape entire sectors overnight (education tech, fintech, gaming, ride-sharing — all  
  preceded by detectable signals)                                                                                                                         
  - State enterprise M&A — when a SOE acquires a private company, it signals strategic priority elevation by the state                                  
  - "Common prosperity" policy language — tracks redistribution vs. growth phase of policy cycle                                                          
  - BRI (Belt and Road Initiative) investment flows — reveals which African/Asian markets China is strategically entering                                 
                                                                                                                                                          
  Why China matters even if the project is EU/FR/US focused:                                                                                              
  - Chinese capital entering a Western market is a competitive signal (acquisition or investment)                                                         
  - Chinese regulatory crackdowns create global supply chain ripples within 30–60 days                                                                    
  - Chinese tech company moves (Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance, Huawei) often precede Western equivalents by 18–24 months

  Africa — Signal Sources and Intelligence Profile                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                          
  Africa is not one market. It is 54 countries across 5 distinct economic zones with radically different regulatory environments, infrastructure states,
  and growth trajectories. The user should specify which African markets or zones are relevant — but the methodology has to be built for the complexity.  
   
  Pan-African Layer                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                        
  Regulatory / Policy:
  - African Union Commission — AU policy directives, continental frameworks
  - AfCFTA (African Continental Free Trade Area) — single market integration progress, tariff changes, protocol updates                                   
  - African Development Bank (AfDB) — investment priorities, infrastructure loan signals                               
  - Afreximbank — trade finance, intra-African trade flows                                                                                                
  - AU Data Policy Framework — continent-wide data governance direction                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                          
  Market Intelligence:                                                                                                                                    
  - Partech Africa — annual venture funding report (best pan-African startup data)                                                                        
  - Briter Bridges — startup ecosystem mapping                                                                                                            
  - African Tech Alliance — policy advocacy tracking                                                                                                    
  - Afrobytes / Africa Tech Summit agendas — what problems are getting investment attention                                                               
                                                                                                                                                        
  Key pan-African signals:                                                                                                                                
  - AfCFTA implementation pace — when a new product category or sector gets added to the free trade framework, it signals an opening market             
  - Mobile money expansion — Africa leads globally on mobile financial services; M-Pesa ecosystem signals often predict fintech plays by 2–3 years before 
  they reach other regions                                                                                                                               
  - Chinese infrastructure investment via BRI — roads, ports, power grids (precedes market entry signals)                                                 
  - Population dividend signals — median age, urbanization rate, internet penetration by country — these are the fundamental long-horizon signals for   
  Africa                                                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                          
  Key African Market Clusters                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                          
  West Africa (Nigeria-led):                                                                                                                            
  - SEC Nigeria — securities regulation                                                                                                                   
  - CBN (Central Bank of Nigeria) — fintech, mobile money, FX policy (very active, high signal density)                                                   
  - FCCPC (Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission) — antitrust signals                 
  - Techpoint Africa / Disrupt Africa — Nigerian tech ecosystem                                                                                           
  - Key industries: fintech (Flutterwave, Paystack ecosystem), energy (oil transition signals), agritech                                                  
                                                                                                                                                          
  East Africa (Kenya-led):                                                                                                                                
  - CMA Kenya (Capital Markets Authority) — securities                                                                                                    
  - CBK (Central Bank of Kenya) — M-Pesa regulatory environment, CBDC signals                                                                             
  - Ethiopia Investment Commission — manufacturing, tech parks                                                                                          
  - Key industries: mobile money (reference market globally), agritech, logistics, climate tech                                                           
                                                                                                                                                          
  North Africa (Egypt / Morocco-led):                                                                                                                     
  - EGX (Egyptian Exchange) — corporate signals                                                                                                           
  - FRA (Financial Regulatory Authority, Egypt) — fintech, insurtech                                                                                      
  - AMDIE (Morocco Investment Agency) — FDI signals, industry zones                                                                                       
  - Key industries: renewable energy (Morocco solar), tech outsourcing, EU nearshoring destination signals                                              
                                                                                                                                                          
  Southern Africa (South Africa-led):                                                                                                                     
  - JSE (Johannesburg Stock Exchange) — most liquid African market, M&A signals                                                                           
  - SARB (South African Reserve Bank) — fintech, payments regulation                                                                                      
  - Competition Commission SA — increasingly active antitrust enforcement                                                                               
  - Key industries: mining (resource cycle signals), fintech, retail, healthcare                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                          
  What makes Africa structurally different as a research track:
  - Leapfrogging dynamics — markets skip infrastructure stages (mobile money before banking, solar before grid power). Standard Western market models do  
  not apply.                                                                                                                                              
  - Regulatory volatility — policies can change with elections or single executive decisions. TTA tags for regulatory signals should be shortened by      
  30–50% vs. EU/US equivalents.                                                                                                                           
  - Currency risk as a signal layer — FX restrictions and devaluation events are market signals in themselves (affects what products can be priced, which 
  competitors can enter)                                                                                                                                 
  - Chinese and Gulf capital presence — BRI investment and Gulf sovereign wealth funds are frequently the primary foreign capital signal in African       
  markets

  Oceania — Signal Sources and Intelligence Profile                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                        
  Australia (AU)

  Regulatory / Policy:
  - ASIC (Australian Securities and Investments Commission) — financial markets, fintech
  - ACCC (Australian Competition and Consumer Commission) — antitrust, digital platforms (one of the most active in the world on Big Tech regulation)     
  - APRA (Australian Prudential Regulation Authority) — banking, insurance, super funds                                                              
  - ATO (Australian Tax Office) — tax policy signals affecting investment structures                                                                      
  - FIRB (Foreign Investment Review Board) — foreign acquisition approvals (especially Chinese investment — highly politically sensitive)                 
  - Treasury Australia — economic policy, digital asset regulation                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                          
  Market Intelligence:                                                                                                                                    
  - ASX (Australian Securities Exchange) — listed company announcements, M&A                                                                              
  - Airtree / Blackbird investor reports — Australian/NZ startup ecosystem                                                                                
  - StartupAUS — policy advocacy, ecosystem state                                                                                                       
  - AFR (Australian Financial Review) — primary business intelligence source                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                          
  Key Australian signal types:                                                                                                                            
  - ACCC digital platform inquiries — Australia frequently leads regulatory frameworks that EU/UK then follow (precedent signal for global tech           
  regulation)                                                                                                                                             
  - FIRB decisions — Chinese acquisition rejections signal geopolitical tension levels and sectors the Australian government considers strategic        
  - Superannuation fund investment shifts — AU super funds are among the world's largest institutional investors; their sector allocation shifts are macro
   signals                                                                                                                                                
  - Mining sector signals — iron ore, lithium, rare earths (critical minerals for battery tech, chips)                                                    
  - Nearshoring signals — Australia as Asia-Pacific HQ for multinationals (follows US policy on China decoupling)                                         
                                                                                                                                                          
  New Zealand (NZ)                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                          
  Regulatory:                                                                                                                                             
  - NZX — listed companies, M&A                                                                                                                         
  - Commerce Commission NZ — antitrust, increasingly active                                                                                               
  - RBNZ (Reserve Bank of New Zealand) — monetary policy, open banking
  - MBIE (Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment) — economic policy, tech strategy                                                               
                                                                                                                                                        
  Key NZ signals:                                                                                                                                         
  - NZ as policy laboratory — small market, fast regulation, often first to implement novel frameworks (carbon zero, digital identity, open banking)    
  - Agritech signals — NZ is a global leader in precision agriculture, dairy tech, food science                                                           
                                                                                                                                                        
  Pacific Islands                                                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                        
  Unique signal profile:                                                                                                                                  
  - Climate-driven investment signals — Pacific Island nations are the primary pressure point for climate finance; what happens here foreshadows global 
  climate policy 3–5 years out                                                                                                                            
  - Development bank signals — ADB (Asian Development Bank), World Bank Pacific programs
  - Subsea cable investment — geopolitical signal for US/AU/CN competition in the Pacific                                                                 
  - Digital inclusion infrastructure — satellite internet rollout (Starlink Pacific) as market entry signal                                               
                                                                                                                                                          
  What makes Oceania structurally different:                                                                                                              
  - Australia punches above its weight on regulatory innovation — ACCC is globally watched                                                                
  - NZ moves fast due to small government and high trust — good test market for new business models                                                       
  - Pacific Island signals are almost entirely climate and sovereignty-driven, not commercial — different lens required
