Complete Brainstorm — Addressing All 15 Gaps + Core Operating Rule
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  The Foundation Rule First — Non-Assumption, Non-Extrapolation                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                          
  This rule overrides everything else in the methodology. It applies to every agent, every layer, at all times.                                         
                                                                                                                                                          
  An agent that fills a data gap with a guess is not doing research — it is writing fiction with citations. The business team never assumes. It never     
  extrapolates beyond what the data explicitly supports. It never chooses between two conflicting signals by picking the one that fits the emerging       
  narrative.                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                        
  When any of the following occurs:                                                                                                                       
  - Two data points from any sources conflict
  - A data gap exists where proceeding would require guessing                                                                                             
  - A pattern could be interpreted two different ways                                                                                                   
  - A causal link is implied but not demonstrated by the data                                                                                           
  - A trend is projected forward beyond the evidence window                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                          
  The agent STOPS. It does NOT proceed. It creates a Conflict Surface Item and adds it to the Conflict Surface List (CSL).                                
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                   
  The Conflict Surface List (CSL)                                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                          
  The CSL is the single most important output of the entire methodology. It is the living record of everything the research doesn't know yet — the honest
  boundary between intelligence and assumption.                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                        
  Every research session produces a CSL. Every CSL is presented to the user before any downstream work continues. The user makes the call on each item. No
   agent resolves a conflict on behalf of the user.

  What qualifies as a CSL item:                                                                                                                         
  - Two sources that directly contradict each other on a factual claim                                                                                    
  - A signal with confidence < 40 that the analysis depends on                                                                                            
  - A trend projection extending more than 6 months beyond the data                                                                                     
  - A "so what" implication that requires an assumption to hold                                                                                           
  - Any finding where a second agent interpreted the same data differently                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                          
  What does NOT go on the CSL:                                                                                                                            
  - Normal uncertainty (all research has uncertainty — this is not a CSL item)                                                                            
  - Two sources covering different time windows or geographies (note the difference, don't conflict-flag)                                                 
  - Subjective differences in framing that don't affect the strategic conclusion

  Gap 1 — Feedback Loops: The Flow Is Not Linear                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                          
  The methodology does not go 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 and stop. Each layer can trigger a re-examination of the layer above it when specific conditions are met.
  These are called upstream invalidation triggers.                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                        
  Upstream Invalidation Triggers                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                        
  Layer 5 → Layer 4 re-run triggered when:                                                                                                                
  - Buyer language reveals a pain category no competitor profile addresses
  - Buyers describe a solution that doesn't appear in any competitor's product                                                                            
  - The dominant buying trigger in Layer 5 contradicts the GTM motion profiled in Layer 4                                                               
                                                                                                                                                          
  Layer 4 → Layer 3 re-run triggered when:                                                                                                                
  - A competitor is found operating in a segment not identified in the Layer 3 map                                                                      
  - Competitive positioning reveals the segment boundary was drawn in the wrong place                                                                     
  - A competitor's TAM language suggests a different market definition than Layer 3 used                                                                
                                                                                                                                                          
  Layer 3 → Layer 2 re-run triggered when:                                                                                                              
  - Segment analysis reveals the market actually belongs to a different sector                                                                            
  - Pricing dynamics found in Layer 3 don't match the margin structure from Layer 2                                                                     
  - The competitive density pattern suggests a different industry classification applies                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                          
  Layer 2 → Layer 1 re-run triggered when:                                                                                                                
  - The structural force identified in Layer 1 demonstrably does not apply to this sector                                                                 
  - The regulatory environment found in Layer 2 contradicts the macro policy assumption from Layer 1                                                      
  - Capital flow patterns in the sector move against the Layer 1 macro prediction                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                          
  Rule: An upstream invalidation trigger creates a CSL item AND pauses forward progress. The user is shown: which trigger fired, what it means for the    
  layer above, and whether to re-run or accept the discrepancy as a noted conflict.                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  Gap 2 — Contradiction Protocol: Formalized                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                          
  Already addressed above via the CSL. Operationally:
                                                                                                                                                          
  When an agent finds a contradiction within its own layer (not just cross-layer):                                                                        
  1. Do not silently reconcile it                                                                                                                         
  2. Do not pick the source you prefer                                                                                                                    
  3. Create a CSL item immediately                                                                                                                      
  4. Continue collecting other signals in the same layer — but mark the finding as blocked until the CSL item is resolved                               
  5. Present the complete CSL to the user before handing the layer output to the next agent                              
                                                                                                                                                          
  No layer output is valid if it contains unresolved CSL items. The user resolves every CSL item before the layer closes.

  Gap 3 — Signal Freshness and Decay Model                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                        
  Every signal recorded in the system carries a freshness tag. Freshness is not the same as date — it is the estimated useful life of the signal given its
   type.                                                                                                                                                  
   
  Decay Rate Table                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                        
  Signal Type                  Decay Rate    Review Trigger                                                                                             
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────                                                                               
  Executive / board change      30 days       New appointment or departure                                                                                
  M&A announcement              60 days       Deal close / collapse event                                                                                 
  Funding round                 60 days       Next round or product pivot                                                                                 
  Community sentiment           30 days       Thread activity collapses                                                                                   
  Regulatory proposal           90 days       Vote, amendment, enforcement date                                                                           
  Market sizing (TAM/SAM)      365 days       Annual report update                                                                                        
  Technology trend             180 days       Product GA launch or abandonment                                                                            
  Buyer psychology             730 days       Major market event resets behavior                                                                          
  Patent filing cluster        365 days       Commercial product launch                                                                                   
  Competitive pricing           90 days       Pricing page change detected                                                                                
  Conference intelligence       90 days       Next event in same domain                                                                                   
  Expert network call          180 days       Market-shifting event overrides                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                          
  When a signal's freshness expires, it moves from Active to Stale in the signal registry. Stale signals cannot be used as primary evidence — they can    
  only be referenced as historical context. A stale signal cited as current evidence is a CSL item.

  Gap 4 — Watchlist: Near-Signals Are Not Discarded                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                          
  Signals that fail the gate check are not discarded. They go to the Watchlist — a holding register for signals with potential.
                                                                                                                                                          
  Watchlist Entry Format                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                          
  Signal ID:     W-042                                                                                                                                  
  Date added:    2026-06-23                                                                                                                               
  Source:        Reddit r/legaltech — 3 posts by 2 accounts
  Tier:          4                                                                                                                                        
  Gates passed:  2 of 5 (Specificity: pass, Reproducibility: fail — only 2 threads)                                                                     
  Missing:       1 more independent source + corroboration from Tier 1-3                                                                                  
  Review date:   2026-07-23                                                                                                                               
  Auto-promote:  when gates passed reaches 3                                                                                                              
  Status:        Watching                                                                                                                                 
  Note:          Complaint pattern matches Layer 3 white space hypothesis — worth monitoring                                                              
                                                                                                                                                          
  Watchlist review cadence: market-researcher checks the watchlist every 30 days. Any item that has gained corroboration is re-evaluated against the gate 
  system. Items on the watchlist for > 6 months with no movement are presented to lead-business for archival or dismissal.

  Gap 5 — Signal Prioritization: The Signal Priority Score                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                          
  When multiple signal clusters fire simultaneously, the following scoring system determines which gets the next deep-dive slot.
                                                                                                                                                          
  Signal Priority Score (SPS)                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                          
  Urgency (time-to-action):   40% weight                                                                                                                  
    Immediate < 30 days        → 40 pts                                                                                                                   
    Watch 30–180 days          → 25 pts                                                                                                                   
    Horizon 6–24 months        → 10 pts                                                                                                                   
    Structural 2–5 years       → 5 pts                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                        
  Magnitude (impact if true):  30% weight                                                                                                                 
    Category-level shift       → 30 pts                                                                                                                 
    Segment-level shift        → 20 pts                                                                                                                   
    Player-level shift         → 10 pts
    Marginal                   → 5 pts                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                        
  Proximity (relevance now):   20% weight                                                                                                                 
    Core to current positioning → 20 pts                                                                                                                
    Adjacent to positioning     → 10 pts                                                                                                                  
    Peripheral                  → 5 pts                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                          
  Confidence:                  10% weight
    Score 80–100               → 10 pts                                                                                                                   
    Score 60–79                → 7 pts                                                                                                                    
    Score 40–59                → 4 pts                                                                                                                  
    Score < 40                 → 1 pt                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                          
  SPS = sum of weighted scores, max 100                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                          
  Clusters scoring ≥ 70 trigger an immediate Layer 2 deep dive.                                                                                           
  Clusters scoring 50–69 go to the 30-day watch queue.                                                                                                  
  Clusters scoring < 50 go to the Watchlist.                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                          
  When SPS scores are equal, lead-business makes the tiebreak call and presents both options to the user if the difference is < 5 points.

  Gap 6 — Adjacent Market Lateral Scan: Layer 1.5                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                          
  Between Layer 1 (macro) and Layer 2 (sector), a lateral sweep is added. This is not a full layer — it is a 20% effort scan that prevents the methodology
   from becoming blind to outside-in disruption.                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                        
  Layer 1.5 — Lateral Horizon Scan                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                        
  Questions:                                                                                                                                              
  - Which adjacent categories serve the same customer but with a different product?
  - Which categories use different customers but the same distribution channel?                                                                           
  - Which technologies currently deployed in other sectors could migrate here in < 3 years?                                                             
  - Who is solving the same problem from a completely different starting point?                                                                           
  - Which startups are explicitly positioning against incumbents in this sector from outside it?                                                          
                                                                                                                                                          
  Sources:                                                                                                                                                
  - YC batch analysis (by problem domain, not by sector label)                                                                                            
  - ProductHunt category overlap                                                                                                                          
  - Cross-industry case study databases                                                                                                                 
  - ArXiv applied papers in non-obvious domains                                                                                                           
  - Job posting language from companies not yet in the sector radar                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                          
  Output: An Adjacent Threat Map — a short list of 3–5 adjacent plays with: origin category, migration vector, estimated timeline, and a risk rating (low 
  / medium / high to current positioning).                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                          
  This runs in parallel with Layer 2, not before it. It feeds the Layer 3 competitive map with a "non-obvious competitors" column.

  Gap 8 — Geographic Lens: FR / EU / US as Parallel Tracks                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                          
  Every layer now runs on three parallel geo-tracks. The global signal is still captured, but it is always decomposed into its geographic variants.
                                                                                                                                                          
  Geo-Track Structure                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                          
  Layer X Finding                                                                                                                                         
  ├── Global:   [what's true across all geographies]                                                                                                    
  ├── FR:       [France-specific variant — regulation, culture, market structure]                                                                         
  ├── EU:       [EU-wide variant — single market effects, regulatory harmonization]                                                                       
  ├── US:       [US-specific variant — market dynamics, regulatory environment]                                                                           
  └── Delta:    [where FR / EU / US diverge and why that matters]                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                          
  When geo-tracks diverge significantly, a CSL item is created. The user decides whether to analyze all three tracks in depth or focus the deep dive on   
  one geography.                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                          
  FR-specific signal sources: AMF, CNIL, Legifrance, Autorité de la Concurrence, INSEE (statistics), DARES (labor), BPI France (public investment bank    
  announcements), Bpifrance Le Hub (startup ecosystem)                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                          
  EU-specific: EUR-Lex, ESMA, EBA, DG COMP, European Commission press, EDPB, European Parliament committee reports                                        
                                                                                                                                                        
  US-specific: SEC EDGAR, FTC/DOJ, Federal Register, Congressional Research Service, State AG actions, CFPB

  Gap 9 — Confidence Scoring: Numerical System                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                        
  Every signal receives a Confidence Score (CS) from 0–100.
                                                                                                                                                          
  Scoring Formula
                                                                                                                                                          
  Base score by tier:                                                                                                                                   
    Tier 0 (private intelligence)    85                                                                                                                   
    Tier 1 (hard evidence)           90                                                                                                                   
    Tier 2 (verified publication)    70                                                                                                                   
    Tier 3 (expert opinion)          50                                                                                                                   
    Tier 4 (community signal)        gates_passed × 10  (max 50)                                                                                        
    Tier 5 (raw social)              gates_passed × 6   (max 30)                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                          
  Modifiers:                                                                                                                                              
    +10    Corroborated by a different tier                                                                                                               
    +10    Source has historical accuracy track record                                                                                                    
    +5     Signal matches a pattern seen in a previous layer                                                                                              
    -10    Single source, no corroboration available                                                                                                      
    -15    Contradicted by another signal (creates CSL item)                                                                                              
    -20    Source has shown prior inaccuracy                                                                                                              
    -25    Signal is more than 50% past its decay window                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                          
  Minimum CS to use as primary evidence:   60                                                                                                             
  Minimum CS to use as supporting context: 35                                                                                                             
  Below 35:                                Watchlist only, never cite in output                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                          
  Every output document shows the CS for each key finding. A finding with CS < 60 used as primary evidence is automatically a CSL item.

  Gap 10 — Time-to-Action Classification                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                        
  Every signal is tagged with a TTA (Time-to-Action) classification at capture.
                                                                                                                                                          
  Immediate   < 30 days     Act or lose the window. Escalate to lead-business same day.
                            Examples: acquisition closing, regulatory enforcement begins,                                                                 
                            competitor launches in your segment                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                          
  Watch       30–180 days   Monitor actively, prepare contingencies, begin planning.                                                                      
                            Examples: funding round closing, proposed regulation in committee,                                                          
                            competitor hiring surge in your domain                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                          
  Horizon     6–24 months   Strategic planning input. Factor into next strategy cycle.                                                                    
                            Examples: regulation in drafting stage, technology crossing                                                                   
                            deployment threshold, demographic shift becoming measurable                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                        
  Structural  2–5 years     Worldview update. Shapes long-term positioning assumptions.                                                                   
                            Examples: macro demographic shift, foundational technology                                                                  
                            becoming infrastructure, geopolitical realignment                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                          
  TTA + SPS interaction: A signal with TTA = Immediate automatically scores at least 40 urgency points in the SPS, regardless of confidence. An           
  unconfirmed signal about an imminent acquisition is still escalated — but always flagged as low-confidence on the CSL.

  Gap 11 — Cross-Layer Synthesis: Layer 6                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                          
  After all 5 layers complete, a synthesis pass is mandatory before any output is delivered to the user. This is Layer 6 — Strategic Synthesis.
                                                                                                                                                          
  Layer 6 — Strategic Synthesis                                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                          
  Owner: lead-business assembles, ceo-advisor writes the executive synthesis                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                        
  Input: All 5 layer deliverables + the full CSL                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                        
  Process:                                                                                                                                                
  1. lead-business lays all layer outputs side by side                                                                                                  
  2. Checks for internal consistency across layers                                                                                                        
  3. Flags any cross-layer conflicts not yet on the CSL
  4. ceo-advisor writes the Strategic Situation Summary                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                          
  Strategic Situation Summary Format (1 page max)                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                          
  THE WORLD AS IT IS (Layer 1 + 2)                                                                                                                        
  [2–3 sentences: the macro force and which sector it lands in]                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                          
  THE MARKET AS IT IS (Layer 3 + 4)                                                                                                                       
  [2–3 sentences: the competitive arena, who's winning, and the exploitable gap]                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                          
  THE BUYER AS THEY ARE (Layer 5)                                                                                                                       
  [2–3 sentences: what the real buyer actually needs, in their language]                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                          
  THE STRATEGIC CONCLUSION                                                                                                                              
  [1 sentence: the single most important thing this research tells us to do]                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                          
  OPEN CONFLICTS (from CSL — unresolved items the user must decide)                                                                                       
  C01: [one-line summary] — awaiting user decision                                                                                                        
  C02: [one-line summary] — awaiting user decision                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                        
  CONFIDENCE IN THIS SYNTHESIS

  RECOMMENDED NEXT ACTION                                                                                                                                 
  [One concrete next step, with rationale. Not a list.   Gap 13 — Failure Mode Detection: The Integrity Check                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                          
  At the end of every layer AND at Layer 6 synthesis, an integrity check runs. If any red flag fires, forward progress stops and the user is notified.
                                                                                                                                                          
  Integrity Check Red Flags                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                          
  Red Flag 1 — Echo Chamber                                                                                                                             
  All sources agree. Zero contradictions found across the entire layer.                                                                                   
  Action: Pause. Report to user. Actively seek a contradicting source before closing the layer.                                                           
  Why: Real markets are contested. Total consensus in research usually means you                                                                          
       only looked at sources that agreed with each other.                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                          
  Red Flag 2 — Too Clean                                                                                                                                  
  The synthesis conclusion matches the opening hypothesis exactly.                                                                                        
  Action: Pause. Present the match to user. Ask: "Did we find this or did we look for it?"                                                                
  Why: Confirmation bias produces research that validates what you already believed.                                                                      
       If the data exactly confirms the hypothesis, check whether disconfirming                                                                           
       sources were silently filtered out.                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                          
  Red Flag 3 — Layer Mismatch                                                                                                                             
  Layer 5 buyer language doesn't match Layer 3 market assumptions.                                                                                        
  (e.g., L3 says buyers prefer subscription pricing; L5 shows buyers are leaving                                                                          
  subscription products for one-time purchase alternatives)                                                                                               
  Action: CSL item + upstream invalidation trigger for Layer 3 re-run.                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                          
  Red Flag 4 — Missed Event                                                                                                                               
  A significant market event (acquisition, regulatory change, product launch)                                                                             
  appears in a later layer that should have been caught in an earlier layer.                                                                              
  Action: Identify the source gap. Add the missed source to the active source list.                                                                       
          Note in the output that early signals on this topic were not captured.                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                          
  Red Flag 5 — Single-Source Synthesis                                                                                                                    
  The strategic conclusion rests on a single primary source.                                                                                              
  Action: Do not publish synthesis. Either find corroboration or downgrade                                                                                
          the finding to a hypothesis on the CSL.

  Gap 14 — Output Audience Segmentation                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                          
  Every deliverable produced by the business team is formatted in the mode specified at task creation. Default: Working Document.                       
                                                                                                                                                          
  Format A — Executive Brief (1 page)                                                                                                                   
    For: CEO, board member, investor meeting prep                                                                                                         
    Contains:                                                                                                                                             
      - 3 headline findings (one sentence each)                                                                                                           
      - 1 strategic implication                                                                                                                           
      - 1 recommended next action                                                                                                                       
      - Open conflicts count (e.g., "2 unresolved conflicts — see Working Document")                                                                      
    What it does NOT contain: sources, methodology, confidence scores                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                          
  Format B — Working Document (5–10 pages)  [DEFAULT]                                                                                                     
    For: internal team, strategy sessions, planning cycles                                                                                                
    Contains:                                                                                                                                             
      - All layer findings with implications blocks                                                                                                     
      - Full CSL with user decisions recorded                                                                                                             
      - Source citations for primary findings                                                                                                           
      - Confidence scores for key claims                                                                                                                  
      - Watchlist summary                                                                                                                                 
      - Strategic Situation Summary (Layer 6)                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                          
  Format C — Deep Dive (full, no page limit)                                                                                                            
    For: investment decisions, regulatory submissions, competitive intelligence archive                                                                   
    Contains:                                                                                                                                             
      - Everything in Format B                                                                                                                          
      - All signal cards with full metadata                                                                                                               
      - Complete source list with tier assignments and freshness status                                                                                 
      - Methodology notes                                                                                                                                 
      - All Watchlist entries
      - Historical conflict log (including resolved items)
