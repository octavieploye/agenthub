5-Layer Research Methodology Brainstorm                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                          
  business team — Eagle View → Niche Expert
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                   
  Layer 1 — Eagle View (Global Strategic Macro)

  Altitude: 40,000 ft. Perspective: geopolitical economist, global capital allocator

  What this layer does:
  Scans the world for forces that will reshape entire categories. Not trends — structural shifts. The kind that make entire industries irrelevant or
  create entirely new ones. No company-level thinking here.                                                                                               
  
  Questions this layer answers:                                                                                                                           
  - What macro forces are reorganizing capital right now?                                                                                               
  - Which technologies are crossing the threshold from experiment to infrastructure?
  - Where are regulatory regimes about to create or destroy value?                  
  - What demographic or behavioral shifts are becoming irreversible?
  - Which geopolitical movements are redrawing supply chains, data sovereignty, or access to talent?                                                      
                                                                                                                                                          
  Signals to scan:                                                                                                                                        
  - Central bank policy, interest rate regimes, inflation cycles                                                                                          
  - AI / climate / biotech / energy — crossing from R&D to deployment                                                                                     
  - Cross-border data regulation (EU AI Act, GDPR propagation, sovereignty mandates)                                                                    
  - Capital flow concentration (where is institutional money actually moving?)                                                                            
  - Labor market restructuring (remote, AI displacement, skills mismatch)                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                          
  Who operates here: ceo-advisor sets the lens, investment-curator flags capital signals, market-researcher sources the raw intelligence                  
                                                                                                                                                          
  Time horizon: 3–10 year relevance window                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                          
  Output: A macro signal map — 5–8 forces, each with a directional arrow (creating / destroying / transforming) and a confidence level                    
                                                                                                                                                        
  Feeds Layer 2 by asking: Which industry or sector does this macro pressure land hardest on?                                                             
                                                                                                                                                        
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  Layer 2 — Industry / Sector View                                                                                                                      
                                  
  Altitude: 20,000 ft. Perspective: sector analyst, industry insider
                                                                                                                                                          
  What this layer does:
  Zooms from global forces into a specific industry and maps how that industry is structurally organized — who holds power, how value flows, where the    
  cracks are.                                                                                                                                             
  
  Questions this layer answers:                                                                                                                           
  - What is the value chain of this industry and who captures the most margin?                                                                          
  - Which incumbents are entrenched and why? What would unseat them?                                                                                      
  - Where is consolidation happening and what is it signaling?      
  - What are the regulatory constraints shaping this sector specifically?                                                                                 
  - Is this industry in expansion, plateau, or decline — and why?                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                          
  Signals to scan:                                                                                                                                        
  - M&A activity, PE buyout patterns, IPO clusters                                                                                                        
  - Margin compression or expansion across the value chain                                                                                              
  - Emerging regulatory frameworks targeted at this sector                                                                                                
  - Labor/skills concentration (where is talent scarce?)                                                                                                  
  - Technology adoption curve within the sector (laggard vs. early adopter)
                                                                                                                                                          
  Who operates here: market-researcher owns this layer, business-analyst stress-tests the data, investment-curator tracks capital movement in the sector  
                                                                                                                                                          
  Time horizon: 1–5 year relevance window                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                          
  Output: A sector intelligence brief — value chain map, power holder analysis, top 3 structural tensions, regulatory landscape summary, capital flow     
  direction
                                                                                                                                                          
  Feeds Layer 3 by asking: Within this sector, which market segment is most exposed to the macro forces identified in Layer 1?                            
  
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  Layer 3 — Market / Segment View                                                                                                                       
                                 
  Altitude: 10,000 ft. Perspective: market strategist, segment specialist
                                                                                                                                                          
  What this layer does:
  Inside the industry, identifies the specific market — the actual competitive arena the client operates in or wants to enter. Maps who is competing, how 
  they compete, and where the white space is.                                                                                                             
  
  Questions this layer answers:                                                                                                                           
  - What is the addressable market and how is it segmented?                                                                                             
  - Who are the real competitors (not just obvious ones — adjacent disruptors too)?
  - What does the competitive positioning landscape look like?                     
  - Where are buyers underserved — what jobs are not being done well?
  - What are the switching costs, and where do they sit in the value chain?                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                          
  Signals to scan:                                                                                                                                        
  - TAM/SAM/SOM sizing with growth rate                                                                                                                   
  - Competitive density per segment (fragmented vs. consolidated)                                                                                         
  - Pricing models in use (subscription, usage, seat, outcome-based)                                                                                    
  - Buyer behavior shifts (self-serve vs. enterprise, PLG vs. sales-led)                                                                                  
  - Review sentiment, churn patterns, NPS signals from public data                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                          
  Who operates here: market-researcher builds the landscape, business-analyst sizes and scores, strategist begins identifying leverage points             
                                                                                                                                                          
  Time horizon: 6 months – 2 years                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                          
  Output: A competitive landscape map — segment breakdown, player matrix (quadrant or positioning map), white space identification, TAM/SAM/SOM table,    
  pricing dynamics summary                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                          
  Feeds Layer 4 by asking: Among the key players in this segment, who is winning and why — and what does that reveal about what buyers actually value?    
  
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  Layer 4 — Competitive / Company View                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                          
  Altitude: 3,000 ft. Perspective: competitive intelligence analyst, brand strategist
                                                                                                                                                          
  What this layer does:                                                                                                                                 
  Gets close to the actual players. Profiles specific competitors in depth — their positioning, messaging, product bets, go-to-market motion, financial
  signals, and vulnerabilities. This is where strategy starts to get concrete.                                                                            
  
  Questions this layer answers:                                                                                                                           
  - What narrative does each competitor tell, and does it match what buyers say?                                                                        
  - Where is each competitor investing (product, geography, partnership, talent)?                                                                         
  - What do real customers say about them — where do they win, where do they fail?
  - What are their structural weaknesses (pricing, support, integrations, culture, debt)?                                                                 
  - How do they acquire customers and at what economics?                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                          
  Signals to scan:                                                                                                                                        
  - Website, pricing pages, case studies, job postings (reveals investment direction)                                                                     
  - G2 / Capterra / Trustpilot reviews (real customer language)                                                                                         
  - LinkedIn follower growth, exec movements, hiring surges    
  - Press releases, funding announcements, partnership announcements
  - SEO/content strategy (what keywords they're targeting, what they're publishing)                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                          
  Who operates here: market-researcher gathers intelligence, business-analyst scores and compares, positioning-expert reads the messaging patterns,       
  strategist identifies the exploitable gaps                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                          
  Time horizon: Now — 12 months                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                        
  Output: Competitor profiles (3–5 key players) — positioning statement, ICP hypothesis, GTM motion, strengths, vulnerabilities, messaging audit. Plus a  
  gap matrix showing where no one is winning yet.                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                          
  Feeds Layer 5 by asking: Given these competitive gaps, what does the buyer we're targeting actually think, feel, and say when they're in pain?          
  
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  Layer 5 — Niche / Expert / ICP View                                                                                                                   
                                     
  Altitude: ground level. Perspective: buyer psychologist, practitioner insider
                                                                                                                                                          
  What this layer does:
  The final zoom. Stops looking at markets and companies and looks at the human being making the decision. Gets inside their head — their language, their 
  fears, their triggers, their mental model of the problem. This layer is where positioning becomes real or stays generic.                                
  
  Questions this layer answers:                                                                                                                           
  - What exact words does this buyer use to describe their problem — not industry words, their words?                                                   
  - What have they tried before and why did it fail them?                                                                                                 
  - What does a good day look like vs. a bad day? What triggered them to look for a solution?
  - Who else is in the room when the decision is made, and what do they care about?                                                                       
  - What is the one thing that would make them trust us immediately?                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                          
  Signals to scan:                                                                                                                                        
  - Reddit threads, LinkedIn comments, forum posts — raw unfiltered buyer language                                                                        
  - Sales call transcripts or win/loss interviews (if available)                                                                                          
  - Expert practitioners — people who live in this problem space daily                                                                                  
  - Niche newsletter content (what practitioners actually read and share)                                                                                 
  - Customer onboarding friction points — where do new users get lost?                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                          
  Who operates here: positioning-expert owns this layer, market-researcher surfaces the raw voice-of-customer data, strategist translates findings into   
  positioning leverage                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                          
  Time horizon: Now — continuous (buyer psychology evolves slowly but does evolve)                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                        
  Output: An ICP profile — buyer archetype, trigger events, job-to-be-done, exact language samples, objection map, decision criteria ranked, trust signals
   that work. Plus a messaging brief that connects Layer 5 language to Layer 4 competitive gaps.                                                        
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                   
  The Flow (How Layers Connect)

  Layer 1 (Macro)
    → identifies the structural force                                                                                                                     
        Layer 2 (Sector)
          → shows which industry it reshapes                                                                                                              
              Layer 3 (Market)                                                                                                                          
                → reveals which segment is exposed / has white space
                    Layer 4 (Competitive)                                                                                                                 
                      → shows who is failing to serve that white space
                          Layer 5 (ICP)                                                                                                                   
                            → reveals what the buyer actually needs                                                                                     
                                → STRATEGY + POSITIONING emerges here                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                          
  Each layer produces a deliverable. Each deliverable is the input hypothesis for the layer below. The lead-business owns the handoffs. ceo-advisor is    
  consulted after Layer 3 and after Layer 5.
