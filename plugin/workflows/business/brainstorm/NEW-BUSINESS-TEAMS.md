  What is not covered — 5 candidates                                                                                                                    
                               
  1. Risk & Resilience team                
                                        
  What's missing: The business team flags risks as a side effect of research. The data team's risk-analyst detects patterns in past data. But nobody does
  forward-looking risk modeling — scenario planning, regulatory exposure mapping, what-if analysis ("if this competitor gets acquired, what happens to our
   position?").                              
                                           
  Agents: lead-risk, regulatory-analyst (legal/compliance per jurisdiction), scenario-planner (what-if modeling), black-swan-watcher (weak signals before
  they become threats)                               
                                         
  Strong case for this — especially for EU/FR regulatory environment (GDPR, EU AI Act, sector-specific). No current team owns this proactively.
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                    
  2. Financial & Revenue Intelligence team                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                        
  What's missing: Nobody owns unit economics, pricing models, or investor readiness. The investment-curator in the business team is thin — it identifies
  opportunities but doesn't model them financially.                                                                                                       
                                                     
  Agents: lead-finance, unit-economics-analyst (CAC/LTV/burn/runway), pricing-strategist (model selection: freemium/subscription/usage-based, price       
  sensitivity by segment), investor-readiness-advisor (pitch narrative, due diligence prep)                                                             
                                                                                                                                                          
  Strong case — pricing strategy alone is a major gap. A wrong pricing model can kill a technically correct product-market fit.
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  3. Legal & Regulatory Intelligence team  
                                                                                                                                                          
  What's missing: Regulatory risk is currently scattered — the business team's geo modules mention regulations as signals, but nobody systematically    
  tracks compliance exposure or jurisdiction-specific legal risk for a given product or market entry.
                                                     
  Agents: lead-legal-intel, compliance-mapper (GDPR, EU AI Act, sector regulations per active geo), ip-watcher (patent landscape, trademark risk),        
  jurisdiction-analyst (cross-border legal implications)
                                                                                                                                                          
  Relevant for your context — if any of the projects in the Optimaeus system eventually touch EU markets, financial services, health, or AI products,   
  regulatory blind spots are existential.                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                      
  4. Partnership & Business Development team                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                        
  What's missing: Nobody identifies and structures strategic partnerships — distribution channels, co-marketing relationships, potential acquirers, or
  complementary tech integrations. The business team sees the competitive landscape but doesn't mine it for BD opportunities.
                                                                                                                                                          
  Agents: lead-bd, partnership-scout (identifies complementary players), channel-bd-analyst (distribution and reseller opportunities), ma-watcher (M&A
  targets and acquirer signals)                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                        
  Medium priority — depends on stage. If projects are pre-revenue this is too early. If there are live products looking for distribution, this becomes    
  urgent.                                                                                                                                                 
                                             
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  5. Product-Market Fit Intelligence team                                                                                                               
                                              
  What's missing: A bridge between "what the market needs" (business team) and "what to build" (which currently goes straight to Hephaestus/dev). Nobody
  systematically assesses whether a product matches a market, validates feature priority against buyer evidence, or tracks PMF signals over time.
                                                                                                                                                          
  Agents: lead-pmf, feature-validator (maps features to ICP trigger events and objections), pmf-signal-tracker (NPS, churn, retention patterns, expansion
  revenue signals), build-vs-buy-analyst                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                        
  Worth considering — this team would sit between the business research and the dev teams, acting as a translation layer for product decisions.
