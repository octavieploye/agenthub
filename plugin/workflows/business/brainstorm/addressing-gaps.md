Critical gap analysis across both documents:                                                                                                            
                                                                
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  What's Missing                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                          
  1. No Feedback Loops — It Only Goes Down                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                        
  The flow is strictly 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5. But intelligence doesn't work that way. A Layer 5 discovery (buyers say X doesn't exist in this market) can     
  invalidate a Layer 1 assumption. A Layer 4 competitor profile can reframe a Layer 2 sector map. There's no reverse path — no mechanism for bottom-up
  invalidation or hypothesis revision.                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                        
  Missing: an upward signal propagation rule — when a lower layer contradicts a higher layer, what triggers a re-run?

  ---
  2. No Contradiction Protocol

  What happens when layers disagree?
  - Layer 2 says the sector is growing
  - Layer 5 says buyers are actively abandoning the category                                                                                              
  - Layer 3 says TAM is expanding but Layer 4 shows all competitors losing revenue
                                                                                                                                                          
  There's no protocol for when layers produce conflicting signals. Currently the methodology would silently pass bad data downstream.                     
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  3. No Signal Freshness / Decay Model                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                          
  A competitive map from 8 months ago is not the same as one from today. A regulation that was "proposed" is now "enforced." There's no concept of:
  - When does a signal expire?                                                                                                                            
  - Which signal types decay fastest? (exec changes: fast. TAM sizing: slow)                                                                            
  - Who triggers a refresh and on what cadence?                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  4. No Watchlist — Signals Below the Threshold Disappear                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                          
  The filtering framework correctly rejects weak signals — but then throws them away. A signal that fails 2 of 5 gates today may pass 5 of 5 in 3 months
  when corroboration builds. There's no watchlist to park near-signals for monitoring.                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                        
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  5. No Prioritization When Multiple Signals Fire Simultaneously                                                                                        
                                                                
  Layer 1 might reveal 6 structural forces at once. The methodology has no triage rule. Which gets the Layer 2 deep dive first? What's the decision
  criteria — urgency, magnitude, proximity to current positioning, investment availability?                                                               
   
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  6. No "Adjacent Market" Lateral Scan                                                                                                                  
                                      
  The methodology zooms in (1 → 5) but never scans sideways. Disruption almost always arrives from an adjacent category. Uber wasn't in the taxi industry.
   Stripe wasn't in banking. There's no systematic method for watching what's happening in the lanes next to the sector being analyzed.                   
   
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  7. No Proprietary / Expert Network Intelligence                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                          
  All sources are public. Missing:
  - Expert networks (GLG, Tegus, Dialectica, AlphaSights) — 45-minute calls with retired executives or domain practitioners. Highest signal density       
  possible, zero noise                                                                                                                                    
  - Conference intelligence — agenda topics, speaker rosters, and fringe conversations at industry events are among the best early-warning systems that   
  exist                                                                                                                                                   
  - OSINT techniques — reverse image search, Wayback Machine snapshots of competitor sites, DNS records, job posting archaeology (what did they hire 18   
  months ago?)                                                                                                                                         
  - Trade association publications — sector-specific, low noise, practitioner-authored                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                        
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  8. No Geographic Lens Built Into the Layers                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                          
  The user explicitly flagged FR / US / EU as distinct contexts, but the layers treat markets as a single global plane. Regulations, M&A patterns, buyer
  psychology, and even which signals exist differ radically by geography. A methodology meant for FR/EU/US needs a geo-filter at every layer — not just in
   the source list.                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                   
  9. No Confidence Scoring
                          
  Signals have tiers (1–5) but no numerical confidence. How do you compare:
  - A Tier 2 signal of marginal relevance                                                                                                                 
  - A Tier 4 signal that passed all 5 gates with 6 corroborating posts
                                                                                                                                                          
  Currently they're both just "signals." There's no way to rank or prioritize competing signals from different tiers.                                     
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  10. No Time-to-Action Classification                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                          
  A regulation with 18 months before enforcement and an acquisition closing in 30 days are both signals — but they demand completely different response
  speeds. Missing: a time-to-action tag on every signal.                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                        
  Immediate (< 30 days)   — act or lose the window                                                                                                        
  Watch (30–180 days)     — monitor, prepare contingencies                                                                                                
  Horizon (6–24 months)   — strategic planning input                                                                                                      
  Structural (2–5 years)  — worldview update                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  11. No Cross-Layer Synthesis Protocol                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                          
  After a full 5-layer pass, there are 5 separate deliverables. Who synthesizes them? What's the format? How do you go from layer outputs to a single   
  coherent strategic recommendation? The methodology produces research — it doesn't produce a conclusion.                                                 
   
  This is the most expensive gap. Without a synthesis step, the business team produces a stack of documents that the user still has to interpret alone.   
                                                                                                                                                        
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  12. No "So What" Step                                                                                                                                 

  Each layer answers descriptive questions. But:
  - "Amazon acquired this company" is a finding
  - "This means that market position X is now undefendable within 18 months" is the implication                                                           
                                                                                               
  The methodology has no explicit step for converting findings into implications. The strategist is closest to doing this, but it's not formalized as a   
  required step at each layer.                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  13. No Failure Mode Detection                                                                                                                         
                               
  When is the methodology producing false confidence? Red flags that should trigger a reset:
  - All sources agree (echo chamber risk)                                                                                                                 
  - No contradictions found across 5 layers (too clean — signals may have been filtered wrongly)                                                          
  - Layer 5 buyer language doesn't match Layer 3 market assumptions (someone is wrong)                                                                    
  - A major competitor just did something completely unpredicted (the scanning missed it)                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                          
  There's no self-audit step built in.                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                          
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  14. No Output Audience Segmentation                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                        
  Who receives the deliverables? A CEO 1-pager, an investor committee 40-page deck, and an internal team brief all need different framing of the same
  findings. The methodology produces raw research outputs but has no guidance on adapting them for different audiences and decision contexts.             
   
  ---                                                                                                                                                     
  15. No Source Maintenance Protocol                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                          
  Sources go offline, get acquired, change quality, start requiring paywalls. There's no process for auditing the source list — who checks that the feeds
  still work, that the sources haven't degraded, that no better source has emerged?
