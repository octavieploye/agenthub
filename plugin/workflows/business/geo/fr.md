## FR-SPECIFIC SIGNAL TYPES
CNIL enforcement action
  Signal: data product or AI product risk — often precedes EU-wide enforcement by 6-12 months
  TTA: reduce by 20% vs. EU standard

BPI funding program opening
  Signal: state backing for a sector = strategic priority signal
  High confidence — government commitment, not just proposal

Comité de filière meeting
  Signal: industry consortium + government alignment = coordinated sector investment
  Source: Ministère de l'Economie press releases

"Plan France 2030" investment axis update
  Signal: 5-year strategic sector priorities — check annually for axis additions
  Source: Elysee / Gouvernement.fr press releases

Social dialogue signals (CGT / CFDT / MEDEF positions)
  Signal: labor regulation direction — what unions and employers are negotiating
  precedes legislation by 6-18 months in labor domain

## FR STRUCTURAL NOTES
French regulation moves faster than EU average on labor and data.
TTA adjustment: reduce all FR regulatory TTA tags by 20% vs. EU equivalents.
State ownership role: BPI, Caisse des Dépôts, and sovereign wealth (BPI Large Ventures)
are active investors — their sector choices are policy signals, not just market signals.
Social Model: labor law changes (35h, télétravail, CDI/CDD rules) affect product-market
fit for HR tech, productivity tools, and B2B SaaS with French enterprise customers.
