# GEO MODULE: Oceania (AU, NZ, Pacific)
Token budget: ~400

## AUSTRALIA (AU)
REGULATORY / POLICY
  ASIC          Australian Securities and Investments Commission — financial markets,
                fintech licensing, crypto regulation
  ACCC          Australian Competition and Consumer Commission — antitrust,
                digital platform regulation (globally watched — often sets precedent)
  APRA          Australian Prudential Regulation Authority — banking, insurance, super funds
  FIRB          Foreign Investment Review Board — foreign acquisition approvals
                (Chinese investment especially scrutinized — geopolitical signal)
  Treasury AU   economic policy, digital asset regulation, tax reform
  ATO           Australian Tax Office — tax policy affecting investment structures

CORPORATE / MARKET
  ASX           listed company announcements, M&A, capital raises
  AFR           Australian Financial Review — primary business intelligence (Tier 2)
  Airtree / Blackbird  AU/NZ startup ecosystem reports and investment data
  StartupAUS    policy advocacy, ecosystem state of play

AU-SPECIFIC SIGNALS
  ACCC digital platform inquiry launch
    Signal: global tech regulation precedent — Australia leads, EU/UK follow in 12-24 months
    TTA: HORIZON for direct AU impact, WATCH for global signal value
  FIRB rejection of foreign acquisition
    Signal: geopolitical risk indicator — shows which sectors AU considers strategic
    Also reveals: US-AU-CN decoupling pace in that sector
  Superannuation (super) fund sector allocation shift
    Signal: AU super funds are among world's largest institutional investors
    Their sector reallocation = one of the most reliable institutional capital signals globally
    Source: APRA super fund statistics, fund annual reports
  Critical minerals policy announcement
    Signal: lithium, cobalt, rare earths — battery/chip supply chain signal
    AU is world's largest lithium producer — its policy affects global EV and tech supply chains
  AUKUS technology sharing announcement
    Signal: defense and deep tech investment signal — quantum, AI, autonomous systems

## PACIFIC ISLANDS
SOURCES
  ADB Pacific programs, World Bank Pacific, Pacific Community (SPC), PIFS
KEY SIGNALS
  Climate finance commitments to Pacific
    Signal: global climate policy pressure signal — Pacific Island nations are primary
    pressure point for climate finance. What happens here forecasts global policy 3-5 years out.
    TTA: STRUCTURAL
  Subsea cable investment (US / AU / CN competition in Pacific)
    Signal: geopolitical competition for Pacific influence
    Fiber cable from which country = which country's tech stack, data laws, and companies follow
  Starlink / satellite internet rollout speed
    Signal: digital inclusion market entry signal for remote and island markets

## OCEANIA STRUCTURAL NOTES
ACCC is a global regulatory bellwether — track its inquiries for signals about where
EU, UK, and US regulation will be in 12-24 months. This is one of the highest-value
signals this geo module provides.

Australian superannuation funds: ~$3.5 trillion AUD under management.
Their sector allocation moves are macro signals, not just local market data.

AU-China trade relationship: barometer for Western decoupling pace globally.
When AU-CN trade tension increases, supply chain diversification signals fire globally.

NZ: small and fast. Use as policy pilot signal. If NZ implements something, larger
markets are likely considering the same framework.
