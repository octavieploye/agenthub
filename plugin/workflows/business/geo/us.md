## US-SPECIFIC SIGNAL TYPES
SEC Form 4 (insider trading)
  Signal: exec buying = confidence in near-term results | exec selling = risk or exit signal
  Source: EDGAR Form 4 filings — real-time

13D filing by activist investor
  Signal: restructuring, spin-off, or leadership change within 6-18 months
  TTA: WATCH (30-180 days typically)

Delaware Court of Chancery decision
  Signal: M&A deal structure precedent — changes how deals are negotiated nationally

State AG action
  Signal: precedes federal enforcement by 12-18 months — high predictive value
  California AG especially influential on tech, data, and consumer protection

SBIR / STTR federal grant award in a domain
  Signal: federal R&D investment = technology the government wants to exist
  Reliable 3-5 year technology development signal

## US STRUCTURAL NOTES
Federal vs. state: two-layer regulatory system. State law often moves faster.
Executive order: can change enforcement priority overnight (TTA: IMMEDIATE when issued).
Antitrust revival: FTC/DOJ under progressive leadership = more aggressive enforcement.
Litigation risk: class action exposure affects product decisions — track securities litigation.
Political cycle: regulatory direction shifts with administration — factor in election years.
