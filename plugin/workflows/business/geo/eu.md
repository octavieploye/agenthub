## EU-SPECIFIC SIGNAL TYPES
Regulation proposed → typically 18-36 months to enforcement (longer TTA than FR/US)
TTA note: use standard TTA tags for EU regulatory proposals.

Infringement proceedings opened
  Signal: enforcement acceleration — member state non-compliance under pressure

"Digital Decade" 2030 targets
  Signal: tech infrastructure investment direction (connectivity, AI, digital gov)

CBAM / ESG disclosure requirements (CSRD, SFDR)
  Signal: compliance burden creating new B2B market for reporting, audit, data tools

EU "strategic autonomy" language in policy
  Signal: protectionism / sovereignty investment in that sector (chips, cloud, health)

Single Market deepening in a sector
  Signal: new cross-border opportunity — regulatory barrier just removed
