## CN-SPECIFIC SIGNAL TYPES
Regulatory crackdown pattern
  Pre-signal sequence: investigative journalism → regulatory inquiry notice → enforcement
  Window between first signal and enforcement: typically 60-90 days
  TTA: reduce all CN regulatory TTA by 50%
  Sectors affected historically: EdTech (2021), fintech (2020-21), gaming (2021),
  ride-sharing (2021), food delivery (2021) — study these for pattern recognition

SOE acquisition of private company
  Signal: state elevation of that sector to strategic priority
  The private company's technology is now state-controlled
  Impact on competitors: they may lose access to that technology or face new SOE competition

"Common prosperity" rhetoric intensity
  Signal: redistribution phase = headwinds for luxury, EdTech, fintech, private tutoring
  Monitor: People's Daily editorial frequency on this phrase

BRI (Belt and Road Initiative) investment announcement in a country
  Signal: Chinese companies will follow into that market within 12-36 months
  Also signals: infrastructure that enables market entry (ports, roads, data cables)

PBOC digital yuan (e-CNY) expansion
  Signal: payment infrastructure displacement of private players
  Also: cross-border e-CNY = geopolitical signal (reduces USD dependency in trade)

CNIPA patent cluster in a technology domain
  Signal: 2-4 year R&D direction indicator for global competitors
  High-volume clusters in AI, battery, semiconductor = priority technology signal

## CN STRUCTURAL NOTES
TTA CRITICAL: CN regulatory changes can occur within days of official announcement.
Reduce all TTA tags by 50% for CN regulatory signals.
CRITICAL DISTINCTION: proposed policy vs. enforced policy gap is very short in CN.
Never assume a CN proposal has the 18-36 month runway of an EU directive.

Cross-geo impact of CN signals:
  SAMR merger decision → affects global deal structure for any deal involving CN assets
  CAC data localization → forces architectural decisions in global tech products
  BRI investment → precedes CN company market entry — monitor for competitive entry signals
  CNIPA patent clusters → global competitors' R&D direction 2-4 years out
