# GEO MODULE: Broader Asia (JP, KR, IN, SG, SE Asia)
Token budget: ~450

## JAPAN (JP)
SOURCES
  TSE (Tokyo Stock Exchange)   corporate filings, governance changes, TSE Prime listings
  METI                         Ministry of Economy Trade and Industry — industrial policy,
                               semiconductor strategy, green tech, defense tech
  JETRO                        Japan External Trade Organization — FDI signals, trade data
  JPO                          Japan Patent Office — robotics, longevity, automotive tech
  Nikkei Asia                  business intelligence in English (Tier 2)
KEY SIGNALS
  METI "priority sector" designation = long-term industrial investment (STRUCTURAL)
  Corporate governance reform compliance = activist investor entry becoming viable
  JPO patent clusters in robotics / longevity biotech = product pipeline signal
  Japan as test market: aging population makes JP the world's first market for longevity tech

## SOUTH KOREA (KR)
SOURCES
  KFTC          Korea Fair Trade Commission — antitrust, Big Tech enforcement
  KOSPI / KOSDAQ listed company filings
  Korea Herald / Chosun  business intelligence (Tier 2)
KEY SIGNALS
  Samsung / SK Hynix capex announcement = global semiconductor supply signal (IMMEDIATE impact)
  K-content export data = cultural economy expansion and soft power signal
  Battery tech patent filings (LG Energy, Samsung SDI) = EV supply chain signal
  KFTC Big Tech investigation = Asian regulatory enforcement wave (precedes others)

## INDIA (IN)
SOURCES
  SEBI          Securities and Exchange Board of India — market regulation
  CCI           Competition Commission — increasingly aggressive on Big Tech
  DPIIT         startup policy, FDI rules, PLI scheme updates
  RBI           fintech regulation, digital payments, CBDC (digital rupee)
  Inc42 / Entrackr  Indian startup ecosystem, funding rounds
KEY SIGNALS
  PLI scheme sector addition = manufacturing investment signal (government incentivized)
  UPI monthly transaction volume = digital payment adoption benchmark (global reference)
  CCI Big Tech investigation = often precedes EU/US action by 6-12 months (watch)
  India Stack expansion = digital public infrastructure signal (identity, payments, health)
  India-China tensions = supply chain diversification signal for global manufacturers

## ASIA STRUCTURAL NOTES
Japan: long decision cycles — once a corporate governance change happens, it is durable.
Korea: chaebols (Samsung, Hyundai, LG) move markets — their capex = sector signal.
India: regulatory unpredictability but enormous market size — high risk, high potential.
Singapore: small but highest governance quality in SE Asia — what MAS does, others follow.
SE Asia: fragmented regulation, fast growth, mobile-first — standard Western models often fail.
