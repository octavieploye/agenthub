# GEO MODULE: Africa
Token budget: ~500

## CRITICAL FRAMING
Africa is 54 countries across 5 distinct economic zones.
Do NOT treat Africa as one market. Always specify the sub-region or country.
When user activates Africa track: prompt for sub-regional focus.
Options: Pan-African / West Africa / East Africa / North Africa / Southern Africa / All

TTA adjustment: reduce all African regulatory TTA tags by 30-50%.
Regulatory volatility varies heavily by country. CN-level speed in some markets.
Currency events (FX restrictions, devaluation) are themselves market signals.
Leapfrogging dynamics: markets skip infrastructure stages — standard Western market
models do not apply. Mobile money before banking. Solar before grid. Mobile-first always.

## PAN-AFRICAN SOURCES
African Union Commission    AU policy directives, continental frameworks, digital agenda
AfCFTA Secretariat          African Continental Free Trade Area — integration progress,
                            protocol updates, sector inclusion changes
African Development Bank    investment priorities, infrastructure loan announcements
Afreximbank                 trade finance, intra-African trade flow data
Partech Africa              annual venture funding report — best pan-African startup data
Briter Bridges              startup ecosystem mapping by country and sector
Disrupt Africa              funding rounds, market analysis
African Tech Alliance       policy advocacy — startup voice at AU level

PAN-AFRICAN SIGNALS
AfCFTA new sector / protocol inclusion = market opening signal (HORIZON TTA)
AfDB infrastructure loan announcement = 3-5 year market access signal (STRUCTURAL)
Mobile money expansion (M-Pesa ecosystem variants) = fintech infrastructure signal
AU Data Policy Framework update = continent-wide data governance direction
BRI investment announcement in a country = CN market entry signal for that country
  (infrastructure precedes business presence by 2-5 years)
Gulf sovereign wealth (UAE, Saudi) investment in African infrastructure = same signal

## AFRICA STRUCTURAL NOTES
Chinese capital: BRI investment = market entry precursor. Where CN builds, CN companies follow.
Gulf capital: increasingly active in fintech, real estate, infrastructure.
Demographic: median age 19-25 — youngest continent — consumer behavior signal differs from all other geos.
Internet penetration: fastest-growing globally — watch country-level data for market activation.
Mobile-first: desktop assumptions invalid. Product-market fit analysis must be mobile-native.
