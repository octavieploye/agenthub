# TIME-TO-ACTION (TTA)

Every signal receives one TTA tag at the moment of capture.
TTA is independent of confidence score — an uncertain signal can still be IMMEDIATE.

## Tags

IMMEDIATE  (< 30 days)
Act or lose the window. Escalate to lead-business same day.
Examples: acquisition closing this month, regulatory enforcement begins,
competitor launches directly in your segment, funding round closes.
SPS urgency points: 40 (maximum)

WATCH  (30-180 days)
Monitor actively. Prepare contingencies. Begin planning.
Examples: funding round closing next quarter, regulation in committee vote,
competitor hiring surge in your domain, executive departure at key player.
SPS urgency points: 25

HORIZON  (6-24 months)
Strategic planning input. Factor into next strategy cycle.
Examples: regulation in drafting stage, technology crossing deployment threshold,
demographic shift becoming measurable, announced M&A pending regulatory approval.
SPS urgency points: 10

STRUCTURAL  (2-5 years)
Worldview update. Shapes long-term positioning assumptions.
Examples: macro demographic shift, foundational technology becoming infrastructure,
geopolitical realignment, sustained policy direction change.
SPS urgency points: 5

## TTA Interaction with Confidence Score
IMMEDIATE + CS >= 60:  escalate and act
IMMEDIATE + CS < 60:   escalate AND create CSL item — flag as low-confidence urgent
WATCH + CS >= 60:      add to 30-day monitoring queue
WATCH + CS < 60:       add to watchlist with 30-day review
HORIZON/STRUCTURAL:    factor into strategy layer regardless of CS
                       (low-confidence long-term signals are still planning inputs)

## Geographic TTA Adjustments
CN regulatory signals:   reduce TTA window by 50% (enforcement can happen within days)
AF regulatory signals:   reduce TTA window by 30-50% (high volatility, varies by country)
FR regulatory signals:   reduce TTA window by 20% vs. EU average
EU regulatory signals:   standard TTA as defined above
US regulatory signals:   standard TTA — but State AG actions often precede federal by 12-18 months
