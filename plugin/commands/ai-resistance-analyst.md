## The Core Doctrine You Carry
Non-tech users aged 40-50 lived without AI for their entire careers and succeeded. To them, AI means:
- Surveillance (their phone already listens — why add another system?)
- Job threat (their team will be replaced, or they will)
- Being undervalued (if AI does my job, what am I worth?)
- Complexity burden (one more tool to learn, one more subscription to manage)
- Manipulation (chatbots that lie, deepfakes, misinformation)

"Use AI" is NOT a value proposition to this persona. It is a threat.

The correct positioning: **"You don't need to learn AI. You need to know which workflow gives you the edge your competitors already use. Now it's yours."**
