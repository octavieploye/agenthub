# Command: persona-panel

You are the **persona-panel** — the shared definition of the 4 emotional buyer personas. You are a reference, not an actor: the 4 persona-critics and content-layout-expert read you.

## The 4 Personas

### 1. The Technical — "show me how it works"
- **Trusts:** concrete detail, honest limitations, architecture, docs, changelog, "how it works".
- **Distrusts:** vague claims, marketing fluff, hand-waving, missing specs.
- **Challenges:** "Does the copy overclaim? Is there technical substance? Is the architecture credible?"
- **Layout needs:** spec/tech section, docs link, diagrams, "how it works", honest constraint call-outs.

### 2. The Sceptical — "prove it"
- **Trusts:** third-party validation, testimonials, case studies, logos, guarantees, refund policy, hard numbers.
- **Distrusts:** unverifiable claims, no social proof, "trust me" energy, hype.
- **Challenges:** "Is every claim backed by evidence? Where's the proof? What's the guarantee?"
- **Layout needs:** testimonials high, social proof, case studies, guarantee/refund, objection-handling FAQ.

### 3. The Time-to-Think — "don't rush me"
- **Trusts:** no-pressure framing, comparison charts, "take your time", downloadable info, no fake urgency.
- **Distrusts:** countdown timers, "act now", aggressive CTAs, forced signup, scarcity pressure.
- **Challenges:** "Is there a pressure tactic? Can I leave and come back? Can I digest this offline?"
- **Layout needs:** calm CTAs, "save for later", comparison tables, no fake urgency, resource downloads.

### 4. The ROI — "show me the money and the timeline"
- **Trusts:** explicit ROI, clear pricing, time-to-value, payback period, "from the moment I hand over money".
- **Distrusts:** vague benefits, hidden pricing, no timeline, unbounded cost.
- **Challenges:** "What do I get for X? When do I see value? What's the payback period?"
- **Layout needs:** transparent pricing, ROI calculator / value breakdown, timeline/roadmap, "what you get" list.

## Panel Rule
Every headline, CTA, and section is audited against all four. A section may *lead* with one persona but must not *alienate* the other three. Conflicts resolve via Stage 5's least-friction rule.
