# Category: Research & Intelligence

Organizing business, marketing, financial analysis and competitive research in Notion.

## When to load

- After business/marketing/financial research agents complete work
- When notion-memory entries have `type: business|marketing|financial`
- After competitive intelligence gathering

## Where it goes

All research goes under **Business & Strategy/** in the workspace:

| Research type | Notion location | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Competitive intel | Business & Strategy / Competitive Intelligence | Database |
| Financial analysis | Business & Strategy / Financial Analysis | Page with tables |
| Marketing strategy | Business & Strategy / Marketing Strategy | Page |
| Revenue & pricing | Business & Strategy / Revenue & Pricing | Page with scenarios |
| Market research | Business & Strategy / Competitive Intelligence | Database entry |

## Competitive Intelligence Database

Columns:
| Column | Type |
|---|---|
| Competitor | Title |
| Category | Select (Direct / Adjacent / Aspirational) |
| Key finding | Text |
| Our advantage | Text |
| Our gap | Text |
| Source | URL or Text |
| Date | Date |
| Relevance | Select (High / Medium / Low) |

## Research Page Format

For longer analyses (financial, marketing strategy):

```
# [Research Title] — [Date]

## Key takeaway
[One sentence a CEO can act on]

## Summary
[3-5 bullet points]

## Detail
[Full analysis, tables, scenarios as needed]

## Action items
[What to do with this information]

## Source
[Where this came from — agent name, memory reference, external source]
```

## Rules

- Always lead with the key takeaway — CEOs read top-down
- Include source attribution — which agent produced this, from what data
- Financial numbers must include assumptions and scenarios (optimistic/realistic/conservative)
- Link to the relevant product page when research affects a specific product
