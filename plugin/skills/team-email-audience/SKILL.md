# Team Email Audience

5-phase team that builds a newsletter or email list strategy from scratch. Maps the audience and their information diet, positions the newsletter against alternatives, designs a 6-month content calendar with weekly topics, writes a complete welcome sequence, and produces growth tactics specific to the niche and audience geography. Never assumes the audience — every decision is grounded in research.

## When to Use

- You want to start a newsletter but do not know what to write about or who it is for
- You have an email list but it is growing slowly and you do not know why
- You need a welcome sequence that converts subscribers into buyers
- You want a 6-month content calendar so you never run out of topics
- Use AFTER `team-business` or `team-marketing` if the audience is not yet validated

## What You Need Before Starting

- Your product, service, or business (what you sell or do)
- The audience you want to reach (even a rough description)
- Your current email list size (even 0 — the strategy changes at different sizes)
- Publishing frequency preference (weekly / biweekly / monthly)

## What This Team Produces

1. **Audience & Niche Map** — the specific reader this newsletter serves, what they already read, what is missing in the market, and the one-line positioning statement
2. **Content Pillar Framework** — 3–4 recurring content pillars with rationale, one example topic per pillar, and the red thread connecting them to your product
3. **6-Month Content Calendar** — weekly topic list for 26 issues with headline drafts, estimated read time, and which content pillar each issue serves
4. **Welcome Sequence** — 5-email welcome series: welcome + promise / first value hit / reader story / product introduction / soft ask. Each email written in full.
5. **Growth Tactics Playbook** — 5–7 tactics ranked by effort vs. reach for this niche, with implementation notes and a 90-day growth milestone target

## Agent Sequence

1. `persona-profiler` — Phase 1: deep audience mapping — who reads, what they already subscribe to, what gap exists, what language they use
2. `channel-strategist` — Phase 2: newsletter positioning vs. alternatives, content pillar framework, red thread to product (runs after Phase 1)
3. `content-creator` — Phase 3: 6-month content calendar from pillar framework (blocked until Phase 2 delivers)
4. `landing-copywriter` — Phase 4: 5-email welcome sequence, written in full (runs in parallel with Phase 3 if context allows)
5. `conversion-reviewer` — Phase 5: stress-tests welcome sequence against open-rate and click-rate conversion frameworks, flags weak subject lines and CTAs

Max 3 agents active at once. Phases 3 and 4 may run in parallel. Phase 5 is blocked until Phase 4 delivers.

## Key Rules

- Audience definition must name a specific person (job title, situation, goal) — "entrepreneurs" is not an audience
- Newsletter positioning must identify at least 2 existing newsletters in the same niche and explain how this one is different
- Content calendar topics must map to real reader problems — not brand announcements or product features
- Welcome sequence must include at least one email that delivers value before asking for anything
- Growth tactics must be specific to the niche and realistic for the current list size (what works at 0 is different from 1,000)
- If the audience is too broad to focus the newsletter, flag it to the user before building the calendar

## How to Invoke

Tell lead-email-audience your business, your target audience (rough is fine), your current list size, and how often you want to publish. Lead will run Phase 1 and present the Audience & Niche Map before calendar or copy work begins.
