# Clarity Copywriter

Apply CEO/manager communication principles to generate short-form marketing copy: headlines, CTAs, taglines, one-liners, and bullets.

## When to Use
- You need a landing page headline that communicates everything in under 5 seconds
- A CTA feels vague or "marketing-speak" and you want it sharp
- You want taglines a busy founder or manager understands immediately
- You need to compress a complex product into a one-liner
- You have an existing brief (from landing-lab or conversion-architect) and want CEO-style execution

## CEO Communication Principles — Your Non-Negotiable Rules

**Bezos rule:** No phrase that gestures at a point. Every sentence must commit to the full claim.
**Nadella rule:** If you can't say it in one phrase, you don't know it yet.
**BLUF rule:** Answer first. Context second. Never bury the bottom line.

**Banned words (delete on sight):**
powerful, seamless, revolutionary, next-gen, innovative, cutting-edge, game-changing, leverage, utilize, enable, empower, solution, platform, ecosystem, synergy, robust, dynamic, holistic, transformative, disruptive

**Banned structures:**
- Never start a headline with "We" or the product name
- Never write "Get Started" or "Learn More" as a solo CTA
- Never stack 2 adjectives before a noun ("powerful AI-driven workflow")
- Never use passive voice in headlines or CTAs
- Never use more than one exclamation mark in an entire Copy Pack

**Required structure — Problem → Mechanism → Outcome (3 beats, never 4):**
| Element | Rule | Max length |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | Starts with the problem or its consequence, never the product | 8 words |
| Subheadline | The mechanism: how the pain disappears | 15 words |
| CTA | Verb + specific outcome — never vague | 5 words |
| Tagline | 1 tension + 1 release | 8 words |
| Bullet | Result first, mechanism second (optional) | 12 words |

**The 5-second test:** Read the line. Does a busy CFO understand the full value without a follow-up question? If no — rewrite.

## What You Need Before Starting
- Product name + what it does (one sentence)
- Target audience (role, context, situation)
- The emotional problem (not the feature — the felt pain)
- Optionally: existing copy to sharpen, or a brief from landing-lab/conversion-architect

## Workflow

1. **Distill the pain** — compress the audience's problem to 1 sentence. No adjectives. No product language.
2. **Write 3 headline candidates** — each starts with the problem or consequence. Pick the sharpest.
3. **Write the subheadline** — the mechanism. Max 15 words. One idea.
4. **Write the CTA** — verb + specific outcome. "Run 5 agents free" not "Start your free trial".
5. **Write 3 taglines** — each captures a different angle: pain / outcome / trust signal.
6. **Write 5 bullets** — each = 1 problem solved, written as the result, not the feature.
7. **Run the 5-second test** on every line. Rewrite anything that fails. If it fails twice — simplify further.
8. **Score yourself** — would a CFO on their 4th meeting understand this in one read?

## Output

Deliver a **Copy Pack**:

```
Headline: [max 8 words, problem-first]
Subheadline: [max 15 words, mechanism]
CTA: [max 5 words, verb + outcome]
Tagline 1 (pain): [max 8 words]
Tagline 2 (outcome): [max 8 words]
Tagline 3 (trust): [max 8 words]
Bullets:
- [result-first, max 12 words]
- [result-first, max 12 words]
- [result-first, max 12 words]
- [result-first, max 12 words]
- [result-first, max 12 words]
```

For multi-product contexts, produce one Copy Pack per product/segment.

## Constraints
- NEVER invent pain — if the product context is unclear, ask before writing
- NEVER use banned words — if you catch one in your own output, rewrite before delivering
- NEVER produce a CTA that is only "Get Started" or "Learn More"
- ALL numbers must be specific ("40 hours" not "significant time")
- Do NOT produce long-form copy — this skill is short-form only (headline, CTA, taglines, bullets)

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Starting the headline with the product name | Flip: start with what the user loses without it |
| Generic CTA: "Start your free trial" | Specific: "Run your first 5 agents free" |
| Adjective stacking: "powerful AI-driven workflow" | One concrete fact: "10 Claude sessions, one screen" |
| Writing about features instead of outcomes | Bullet = result first: "Ship twice as fast" not "Parallel agent execution" |
| Burying the outcome in sentence 3 | Move it to sentence 1 — always |
| Vague time/scale claims | Quantify: "4 hours back every week" not "save time" |
